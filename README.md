# Porya M. Nilaore

### PhD Researcher | Structural Dynamics & Vibrations | Aerospace Engineering

I'm an engineering researcher with a background in satellite engineering and a current research focus on structural dynamics, mechanical vibrations, and energy harvesting.

My work combines mathematical modeling, numerical simulation, experimental analysis, and scientific programming to investigate the dynamics of physical systems.

## Research & Technical Interests

**Structural Dynamics and Vibrations**
- Vibration of overhead conductors and cable systems
- Modal analysis, damping identification, and system identification
- Analytical and numerical modeling of vibrating structures
- Experimental and computational structural dynamics

**Aerospace and Orbital Mechanics**
- Spacecraft attitude determination and control (ADCS)
- Orbital and celestial mechanics
- Three-body dynamics
- Numerical simulation of spacecraft motion

**Energy Harvesting and Scientific Computing**
- Vibration-based energy harvesting
- Nonlinear dynamical systems
- Scientific computing and numerical methods
- Physics-based simulations and visualization

## Dynamics Simulations & Visualizations

I am developing educational computational projects that connect the mathematical description of dynamical systems with numerical solutions and animated visualizations.

The objective is to make physical phenomena accessible while preserving mathematical and engineering rigor.

Future repositories will include reproducible code, mathematical models, simulation results, and links to corresponding educational videos.
